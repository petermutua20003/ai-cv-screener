JOB VACANCY: DRIVER
Position Title: Driver

Location: Kenya

Job Type: Full-time

Key Responsibilities
Drive company vehicles safely to transport staff, clients, and goods.

Maintain clean, well-serviced vehicles and handle daily inspections.

Keep accurate daily work tickets, trip logs, and fuel records.

Perform basic computer and clerical data entry tasks as needed.

Ensure strict adherence to NTSA rules and Kenyan traffic laws.

Qualifications & Requirements
Education: Bachelor’s degree in any field.

Driving License: Valid Kenyan Driving License (Class B, C, or BCE) with 3+ years of clean driving experience.

Computer Skills: Proficient in basic computer applications (MS Office, data entry, email).

Document: Valid Certificate of Good Conduct from DCI.

Skills: Excellent knowledge of Kenyan roads, basic vehicle mechanics, and strong English/Swahili communication skills.
